# Pocket Billboard privacy policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Pocket Billboard (shown as PocketBoard on your Home Screen) is made by Maira
Khan. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app on
iPhone.

**We do not collect your data.** The app has no account, no analytics, no
advertising and no tracking. It does not send your messages, QR codes,
templates or settings to us, and everything except online dictation works
fully offline.

**What is stored on your iPhone.** Your current message or QR code (the link or
text it holds and its caption), its appearance settings (font, colours, list
style and effect) and your Frequent Templates are saved in the app's own
storage on your device so they are there next time you open the app. The
app's widgets read your templates from the same storage on your iPhone. Like
other app data, this may be included in your iPhone's backups (for example
iCloud Backup or a computer backup) according to your own backup settings. We
cannot access those backups.

**Deleting your data.** Delete individual templates in Frequent Templates, or
delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone.

**Dictation.** If you tap the microphone, the app asks for Microphone and
Speech Recognition permission. When you are online, what you say is sent to
Apple's speech recognition service to turn it into text; Apple's privacy
policy applies to that processing. When you are offline, recognition happens
on your iPhone where supported. The app does not store recordings or
transcripts — only the resulting message text, as described above. You can
turn these permissions off at any time in Settings.

**QR codes.** QR codes are made on your iPhone. The app does not send the link
or text in a QR code anywhere; it only leaves your phone if someone scans the
code from your screen.

**Other permissions.** The app does not request access to your camera,
location, contacts, photos or other personal data.

**Children.** The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone,
including children.

**Contacting us.** If you contact us through the support form, your message
and anything you choose to include (such as an email address for a reply) are
received through Google Forms and used only to answer you. Google's privacy
policy applies to the form.

**Changes.** If a future version changes how information is handled, we will
update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.

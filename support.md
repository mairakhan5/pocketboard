# Pocket Billboard support

Pocket Billboard (PocketBoard on your Home Screen) shows a short message or a
QR code full-screen so you can communicate when speaking is inconvenient. It
works offline and needs no account.

## Getting started

1. Type or dictate (microphone button) up to 60 characters.
2. Tap **→** or slide left to show the message full-screen; slide back or tap
   **←** to edit. The green **+** starts a new board (with Undo).
3. Use the toolbar: **Aa** for font and bullets/numbers, the colour button for
   text and background colours, the light button for flashing and effects, and
   the bookmark for Frequent Templates.
4. On the board, tap to show or hide controls. **Rotate** turns the message 90°;
   pause stops any effect. Pinch to resize the message.
5. To show a QR code, switch the composer from **Text** to **QR** and paste a
   link (for example your Instagram or website), with an optional caption.

## Common questions

- **What does "Low contrast" mean?** The text and background are close in
  lightness and may be hard to read from a distance. You can still use them.
- **Is flashing safe?** Flashing always stays below three flashes a second, and
  effects are softened when Reduce Motion is on. You can pause effects at any time.
- **Dictation doesn't work offline.** Offline dictation depends on your iPhone
  and language. You can always type instead.
- **Why was my pasted text shortened?** Messages are limited to 60 characters;
  the app keeps the first 60 and tells you when this happens.
- **Why is my QR code black and white?** Black on white scans most reliably.
  The screen also goes to full brightness while a QR code is showing, and back
  to normal when you leave it.
- **Where are my templates stored?** Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app
  deletes them. See the [privacy policy](privacy).
- **How do I add the widgets?** Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap **Edit →
  Add Widget**, and choose PocketBoard. Touch and hold a Frequent Templates
  widget and choose **Edit Widget** to pick which templates it shows.
